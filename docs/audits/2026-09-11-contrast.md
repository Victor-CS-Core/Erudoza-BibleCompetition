# Gray text on green: contrast audit — 2026-09-11

The gray-on-green appearance reported by the user and present in the production library capture was reproduced as a **temporary contrast failure during button selection**. When motion was allowed, `.ds-button` transitioned foreground and background colors together while switching between light and dark variants. Intermediate colors approached the same luminance: an isolated reproduction reached **1.24:1**, and real coach-library/student True/False selections reached **1.315–1.329:1**. Settled selected colors measured **5.09:1**, and hovered selected colors **5.882:1**.

[WCAG 2.2 SC 1.4.3](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/contrast-minimum.html) requires at least **4.5:1** for normal text and **3:1** for qualifying large text. Inactive controls are exempt from that criterion; their readability was still improved here. These are scoped measurements, not an application-wide WCAG certification.

## Change and preserved behavior

The fix is confined to [the shared design-system CSS](../../apps/web/src/styles/design-system.css):

- Remove `.ds-button` from the simultaneous foreground/background transition rule. Selection and hover colors switch without the low-contrast intermediate frames.
- Replace disabled-button `opacity: .46` with the existing muted-ink, soft-surface, and strong-border tokens. The computed text contrast is **4.685:1**, while native disabled behavior remains in place.

Existing muted text on the selected surface measures **4.677:1** and passes. The selected/hover palette and other passing color pairs are retained; this is not a broad palette change.
